KinKeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

KinKeep is an app that helps you stay in touch with the people who matter to you. It is published by [your name]. Questions: [your email].

The short version

Everything KinKeep knows stays on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your information anywhere. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. There are no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

What is stored on your phone

  • People you choose to keep in touch with: a copy of the name, phone numbers and birthday from your contacts, and the circle and reminder rhythm you pick for each person.
  • Conversations: records of calls and chats you log in the app (date, direction, length and an optional note). In the direct-download version only, KinKeep also reads your phone's call history to record when you spoke to people in your contacts (time, length and direction). It does not read call content, text messages or numbers that are not in your contacts.
  • Notes you write about a person or a conversation. They are encrypted on your phone with a key held in the Android Keystore.
  • Settings and reminder history such as your quiet hours and which reminders were sent.

Permissions

  • Contacts: so you can choose who to keep in touch with.
  • Call history (direct-download version only): to work out when you last spoke to each person.
  • Notifications: to send reminders.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock: only if you turn on app lock, through Android’s own prompt. KinKeep never receives your biometric data.
  • Start after restart: so reminders continue after your phone reboots.

Backups

You can create a backup file. It is encrypted with a password you choose and saved where you choose. KinKeep and its publisher never see it. If you lose the password the backup cannot be recovered.

Error log

If the app crashes, it keeps a short record on your phone with the type of error and the code location. It does not contain names, numbers, notes or messages. It is never sent automatically. You can share it with the developer yourself, or clear it, from Settings.

Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone, because we do not have it. If you choose to share an error log or a backup, that is your decision and goes only where you send it.

Deleting your data

Open Settings and choose Delete all data to remove everything KinKeep has stored, including the encryption key. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Your phone’s own contacts and call history are never changed or deleted by KinKeep.

Children

KinKeep is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to collect.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
